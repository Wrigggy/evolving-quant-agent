\section{Limitations}

The framework can select an inappropriate economic interpretation, mistake
sensitivity for correctness, or inherit an overbroad rule.  A local experiment
may fail to distinguish relevant alternatives.  These are risks in the
learning mechanism, not automatic reasons to forbid action; they require that
conditions, unresolved explanations, and later outcomes remain visible.
A public paper's wording and a numerical example that separates definitions do
not reveal a hidden evaluator's intended equation, establish official
correctness, or identify a score cause.  Likewise, changing an independently
required upstream quantity cannot be reported as payoff or final-performance
gain when the observed downstream signal and portfolio remain unchanged.

The distinction from generic harness evolution depends on the operational
contribution of quantitative reasoning.  The framing alone establishes neither
novelty nor comparative superiority.  Same-panel adaptive improvement is not
unseen transfer, and one final realization is not a stability estimate.
Restricted benchmark inputs and deterministic reproduction tasks cover only
part of professional quantitative research.  This study neither reproduces a
many-researcher nonstandard-errors experiment nor assumes that ambiguity is the
dominant cause of benchmark failure.
The completed BacktestBench initial-versus-frozen comparison (14/20 to 16/20)
uses 20 fixed instances drawn by UUID and public-text exclusion from the
released test split, with one original Worker draw per package per arm.  It
does not constitute a benchmark-wide official submission, an AutoBacktest
pipeline evaluation, or a repeated-run stability estimate.  Two gains and zero
regressions across 20 tasks is one complete positive observation; it does not
establish that the same gain would replicate under independent sampling,
transfer to a different task family, or generalize beyond backtesting
measurement conventions.  The promoted revision is prompt-only, changing
evaluation scoping from full-window daily MTM P\&L to window-only indicators
and realized round-trip PNL; it does not add an executable tool or validator
that deterministically enforces this policy.  Consumer checks confirm
fresh-\Worker{} uptake in four cases including one zero-score negative, but
same-ledger checks are not independent simulation, and one awarded candidate
retains a residual-cash accounting defect.  The category-balanced panel uses
UUID and public-text exclusions without metadata proving template, market-window,
or latent-lineage disjointness.

QuantCodeEval results remain mixed and do not corroborate the BacktestBench
gain.  The closed twenty-task checkpoint gives binary 2/9 on paired QCE tasks;
the Pro \Evolver{} fork gives QCE binary 2/4 to~3/4 with a T18 two-check
regression and QF 4/4 ties.  This is consistent with the conditional nature
of research-method improvement --- a backtesting measurement policy need not
transfer to QCE tasks requiring different research operations --- but it means
the current evidence does not establish cross-benchmark stable benefit.  The
BacktestBench development screen is a small fixed panel with single original
draws per package; earlier BacktestBench development pairs (8/12 to 11/12
on supervised development and validation tasks) used overlapping task sets
and cannot substitute for the initial-to-frozen endpoint.

Earlier negative results are retained and their scope is unchanged.  The
previous BacktestBench initial-to-frozen endpoint (9/20 to 7/20) used different
task instances and is not superseded by the positive result on a distinct 20.
The operation-family candidate remains partial: T24 and T26 have no delivered
measurement, and jointly measured gains cannot substitute for a full endpoint.
Selected original research units and native-interface documentation are
investigator assistance, not learned capability; historically measured
validation tasks withheld from E are not newly unseen data.

The September 25 six-task QuantCodeEval result is one fixed panel of single
original task realizations, not a repeated-run estimate or significance test.
Whole E13 is selected on T01/T12/T18 development outcomes before the
T19/T26/T27 cells are opened, but those evaluation tasks are historically
exposed rather than sealed held-out data. Their three aggregate scores tie H0,
and T26 trades one type-A pass for one type-B pass, so equal totals do not mean
identical properties or no regression. H0 T18 requires a separately registered
delivery recovery; E13 T18 uses an exactly reconstructed original-journal
source after archive failure; both unsuccessful deliveries remain part of the
record. Generation and official evaluation settings match, but the later
Workers use a 128-file post-generation collection allowance rather than H0's
20-file allowance, with the same 32-MiB cap. The comparison therefore supports
a qualified whole-package artifact-quality pilot, not identical end-to-end
delivery reliability, unseen transfer, stability, or completion of the broader
QFBench/cross-benchmark experiment.

Memory relevance and executable uptake remain implementation risks.  An
archive can accumulate misleading advice; a larger tool library can worsen
selection.  The system must revise and remove experience rather than equate
growth with learning.  In the September 25 pilot, E13--E17 retain empty
operation memory and make zero memory-interface calls, so the six-task result
does not test a memory benefit.  A full failed episode is larger and more structured
than a generic outcome summary; whether the fixed \Evolver{} uses that evidence
well remains an empirical question. Provider interruptions can prevent even
an otherwise supported revision from reaching a decision or fresh Worker.
Such interruptions are neither candidate losses nor successful abstention.
Current bounds apply to selected-history projection and memory bootstrap,
not total per-round evidence, checking or evaluation work. Finally, system-level
self-improvement does not imply recursive improvement of fixed $G$ or the
underlying foundation models.
