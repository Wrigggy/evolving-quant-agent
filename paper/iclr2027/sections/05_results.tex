\subsection{Existing Mechanism Evidence}

\Measured{A public-definition-bound quantity invariant produced
\TwelveInvariantTarget{} on the target task in two independent Worker runs and
kept the T19 protection task at \NineteenProtection{}.} This supports the value
of binding executable checks to public quantity semantics. It does not establish
autonomous invariant synthesis, broader transfer, or superiority to generic
harness evolution.

\Measured{An Evolver-produced R3 candidate harness later generated a fresh T26
artifact that passed \TtwentysixFreshCandidate{} official properties and
received reward \TtwentysixFreshReward{}.} The Worker started from the public
task rather than the prior seed artifact. The probe, promotion, and final
evaluation were still externally arranged, so this is a candidate-harness
feasibility result rather than a complete autonomous search campaign.

\Measured{In the AP-2M r4 rerun, the Evolver autonomously synthesized and
admitted an executable semantic-invariant checker, selected and launched a
Worker repair probe, consumed the probe's missing-artifact outcome, and made a
distinct second \textsc{Act/Refine} decision.} Across the two Evolver rounds
and Worker probe, the run used \APTwoMRequests{} model requests and cost
\APTwoMCost{}. The second decision preserved the uncertainty of the delivery
failure while finding new quant-specific counterevidence: the first checker

invoked the checker, and the second candidate lacked a final-version tool

warm-history feedback-driven autonomy feasibility, not component benefit or
benchmark improvement.
