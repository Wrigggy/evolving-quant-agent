\subsection{Existing Mechanism Evidence}

\Measured{A public-definition-bound quantity invariant produced
\TwelveInvariantTarget{} on the target task in two independent Worker runs and
kept the T19 protection task at \NineteenProtection{}.} This supports the value
of binding executable checks to public quantity semantics. It does not establish
autonomous invariant synthesis, broader transfer, or superiority to generic
harness evolution.

\Measured{An Evolver-produced R3 candidate harness later generated a fresh T26
artifact that passed \TtwentysixFreshCandidate{} official properties and
received reward \TtwentysixFreshReward{}.} The Worker started from the public
task rather than the prior seed artifact. The probe, promotion, and final
evaluation were still externally arranged, so this is a candidate-harness
feasibility result rather than a complete autonomous search campaign.

\Measured{In the AP-2M r4 rerun, the Evolver autonomously synthesized and
admitted an executable semantic-invariant checker, selected and launched a
Worker repair probe, consumed the probe's missing-artifact outcome, and made a
distinct second \textsc{Act/Refine} decision.} Across the two Evolver rounds
and Worker probe, the run used \APTwoMRequests{} model requests and cost
\APTwoMCost{}. The second decision preserved the uncertainty of the delivery
failure while finding new quant-specific counterevidence: the first checker
fixed gamma and used synthetic windows inconsistent with the public CV and
sample-window clauses. The Worker never wrote the requested artifact or
invoked the checker, and the second candidate lacked a final-version tool
smoke, so there was no final Worker or official candidate score. This supports
warm-history feedback-driven autonomy feasibility, not component benefit or
benchmark improvement.

\subsection{Certificate and Quant-$H_0$ Bootstrap}

\Measured{In QEC-1, generic and quant-evidence-certificate Reviewer arms used
the same three cases, audits, model, and two-stage call budget. Both selected
all three intended audits, resolved two cases, and returned calibrated
insufficiency on the ambiguous case.} Neither arm eliminated the holdings-
timing alternative in the transaction-cost case. The certificate produced no
paired improvement or regression, so its mechanism gate was not positive and
it was not inserted into AP-3.

\Measured{AP-3 began with a fresh Quant-$H_0$ Worker at
\APThreeHZero{} on T26. Round one autonomously created and smoked an artifact-
check tool and selected a from-scratch Worker probe. The probe used 11 requests
but produced no artifact. Round two cited the missing-artifact observation,
relocated the mismatch to Research Artifact and Completion, and returned a
calibrated abstention.} The end-to-end run used \APThreeRequests{} requests and
cost \APThreeCost{}. No final candidate or final Worker was admitted, so this
supports bootstrap-loop feasibility but not component or benchmark benefit.

\Measured{A separate bounded AP-3 intermediate probe reused the same-run
Quant-$H_0$ artifact and the round-one candidate. The complete task was visible,
but the live deploy lacked the committed output-prestage helper. The Worker
copied the data backup itself and invoked the candidate checker on its final
model request. The checker returned normally; the runtime then exhausted its
iteration budget before a reconciliation or edit turn. The unchanged artifact
remained \APThreeActivationScore{} with reward zero.} The seven-request probe
cost \APThreeActivationCost{}. It establishes component reach, but deployment
drift prevents a claim about the complete v2 intervention, component
helpfulness, or a fresh candidate harness.

\Measured{The paired r2 changed only the synchronized seed-prestage runner.
The seed was present at the promised output path on the first turn, the checker
ran on request five of seven, and two later requests reconciled its warnings and
attempted functional checks. The artifact nevertheless remained identical to
the seed and official T26 remained 12/17 with reward zero.} The run cost
\APThreeActivationRtwoCost{}. This validates pre-stage, activation, and
post-audit reconciliation, but not diagnostic coverage or component benefit.
