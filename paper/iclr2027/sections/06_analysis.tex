\section{Analysis}

The current evidence motivates, but does not yet validate, the proposed method.
First, a green free-form probe can be self-confirming: executable relations must
be bound to the public quantity or process semantics. Second, component
registration and local smoke success are insufficient; later trajectories must
show actual activation and the predicted invariant transition. Third, invariant
repair and complete task success are different levels of evidence. A component
may correct a temporal or accounting relation while another unresolved error
keeps the official reward at zero.

AP-2M now establishes a narrow feedback-driven loop result. Round one moved
beyond prompt editing to an executable quantitative audit; round two indexed a
real Worker failure, maintained competing explanations, and refined the audit
after detecting public-clause inconsistencies. This is useful evidence that
runtime experience can alter a later harness decision. It also exposes two
remaining gaps. First, the answer-free probe summary reported
\texttt{missing\_artifact} but omitted the process fact that the Worker spent
its short budget reading and never invoked the component, making direct failure
attribution underidentified. Second, final component-smoke orchestration still
had an early-final branch, repaired after the run but not yet live-verified.
The repair probe also supplied its seed under the data directory while the
Evolver-authored instruction described it as already present at the output
path; the post-run setup now pre-stages the seed consistently.
Neither local audit correctness nor a second Evolver decision shows that the
component changes Worker behavior or official benchmark performance.

The proposed invariant signature may improve search in two ways. It can reduce
the ambiguity of sparse official outcomes, and it can index experience by a
recurring quantitative relation rather than a task identifier. Conditional
routing may also reduce interference by activating temporal, portfolio, or
artifact components only when applicable. These hypotheses remain prospective
until QI-1/QI-2 and matched baselines are measured.

QEC-1 did not show that the proposed certificate representation improves a
strong generic structured Reviewer. Both representations chose the same audits
and reached the same case-level conclusions. The certificate remains a useful
open evidence format, but the current data do not justify making it a mandatory
search or promotion signal.

AP-3 closes a narrower autonomy gap. Starting only from a fresh Quant-$H_0$
trajectory, the Evolver selected a non-prompt component, authored a real
from-scratch probe, consumed its failure, changed the located Research State,
and abstained when the observation did not identify a justified repair. This
is stronger than warm-history AP-2M for bootstrap provenance, but weaker for
benefit: the Worker did not deliver an artifact and no final candidate was
evaluated. Round two also lacked the round-one prediction record, so the run
tested observation-conditioned updating rather than the complete predeclared
prediction-versus-outcome contract.

The bounded intermediate probe verifies the task-visibility repair and
component reach, but not the intended seed-staging intervention. A stale remote
Worker runner left the output empty, so the Worker spent part of the short
probe recovering the seed from its backup before invoking the audit on the
last request. The next valid comparison is therefore the same bounded probe
with the committed runner synchronized. Only if activation is still too late
under that corrected setup should the mechanism reserve a post-audit turn.
This result does not support increasing all Worker budgets or claiming that the
component improves T26.

The paired r2 removes that deployment ambiguity. Once pre-stage was active, the
Worker reached the checker earlier and had two post-audit requests, so terminal
timing is no longer the limiting explanation. The checker reported only legal
temporary join-key warnings and did not distinguish the remaining official
failures; the Worker therefore retained the seed. The next causal test should
change diagnostic coverage through Evolver refinement while holding the blind
Worker probe fixed, rather than adding a general budget increase.

QDR-1 confirms the first half of that prediction. Answer-rich optimize evidence
changed the Evolver's search from a generic artifact audit to an executable
quantitative-consistency component, and a controlled perturbation established
that the component can discriminate the selected mechanism locally. It did not
change Worker behavior. Neither the six- nor ten-iteration blind Worker called
the registered tool; both spent their budgets on broad shell-based inspection
and preserved the 12/17 seed. The second result is important because it rules
out the smallest budget as the sole explanation. The next mechanism should
change when reconciliation is activated or how the Worker selects the
task-conditioned audit, rather than increasing the generic budget again. Until
that transition is observed, local component discrimination must remain
separate from Worker helpfulness and official benchmark gain.

The directed follow-up narrows the activation gap without closing it. The
Evolver twice inferred that a finalization-time audit was too late and authored
a task-conditioned early-call instruction. That intervention changed observed
tool use from zero calls in both QDR-1 probes to one call, but the Worker still
expanded ``bounded inventory'' into five turns of code, paper, and data
inspection. The component then produced actionable errors on turn six, yet the
remaining turn was spent rereading rather than editing. This separates tool
availability from timely Research-State transition and from action on an audit
observation. Another longer prompt or generic budget increase is therefore a
weak next test. A more discriminating comparison is one generic one-shot
checkpoint after a fixed small inventory versus a simplified component call
surface, with first-call position, next action, edit/re-audit, artifact delta,
property delta, and reward measured separately.

P-v2 resolves this mechanism-localization question. The fixed inventory bound
moved the first component call from turn six of seven to turn three of twelve,
and the post-observation reserve allowed the Worker to convert two actionable
findings into an edit, real-data smoke, clean re-audit, and a two-property
official gain. The middleware supplied phase control, not task answers: the
Worker still decided applicability, authored all relation arguments, performed
the repair, and verified the result. Thus component availability was not the
main remaining defect; the prior probe lacked an explicit Research-State
transition and enough causal distance after observation. The next question was
whether the same harness changes a fresh Worker trajectory.

The fresh T26 confirmation closes the next causal gap. Unlike P-v2, no previous
strategy was available, and the Worker activated the component before the
middleware checkpoint could force a decision. The candidate prompt therefore
changed the Worker's construction policy rather than merely enabling a seeded
repair. The audit also had behavioral consequences: the Worker revised the
artifact and reached a clean re-audit before the official net gain. However,
the B7 regression shows that a clean declared-relation certificate is not a
complete correctness certificate. Promotion still needs official properties
and, eventually, repeat or matched-task evidence.

The same run exposes a scaling defect. The Worker declared completion on turn
27, but completion middleware continued requesting redundant transition
smokes through turn 40. Those calls did not establish the causal finding and
inflated cost and latency. This does not invalidate the 15/17 result, but the
terminal condition should stop after artifact delivery, a clean post-edit
audit, and one focused completion check before the method is coordinated over
multiple tasks.

AP-3 r4 does not update the autonomy conclusion. It reached a fresh H0 score
and executed round-one evolution, but a macOS metadata sidecar in the Linux
deploy blocked candidate-history ingestion. The failure was repaired at the
source-transfer boundary and rerun under a new identity; it is neither an
Evolver abstention nor evidence that the proposed search failed.

AP-3 r5 closes the remaining orchestration gap in the bounded protocol. The
Evolver generated a new quant-research skill from the run-local H0 trajectory,
the Worker actually loaded it, and a later Evolver decision used the predicted
versus observed outcome. The calibrated abstention is informative: the probe
showed that passive skill activation did not cause an edit, but it did not test
the competing numerical conventions. The system therefore updated the
intervention-level hypothesis without pretending to have identified the hidden
property cause.

The remaining problem is now more specific than missing feedback plumbing.
MT-1 shows that runtime-conditioned refinement can change executable harness
code, activate it in a fresh Worker, and coincide with an official binary
target gain. However, the short autonomous probes never reached delivery,
whereas the successful confirmation needed 50 Worker turns. Fixed 10--12-call
probes therefore had insufficient causal distance to estimate final candidate
capability.

The protection regression also separates local mechanism value from reusable
harness value. The validator helped the target Worker enforce strict surface
contracts, but its nearby-task run still missed two interior local-vol checks
and a Monte Carlo vanilla-price consistency check. A future candidate must
combine artifact-state validation with a bounded completion/stopping policy
and refine its task applicability from protection evidence. Broad scheduling
remains deferred: evaluating more tasks with the current candidate would scale
cost before resolving its measured scope and stopping defects.

\Boundary{This result is sufficient for a local mechanism-existence claim, but
not for stable transfer or final benchmark performance. A frozen multi-task or
sealed final campaign has not been run.}

MT-2 narrows the breadth question. The outer loop did not collapse to one
local-vol-specific mutation: independent trajectories produced distinct
temporal-state, calibration, and dependence-integrity components, and one new
target family showed an activated four-property gain. Calibrated abstention on
the underidentified FX residual is also useful; forcing every pair to mutate
would inflate candidate count without improving causal resolution.

The limiting factor is now selection efficiency. Two short probes ended before
delivery but later normal-budget Workers completed and activated their
components, while another short Worker delivered artifacts without using its
tool. A short probe is therefore informative about activation and completion
distance, but is not a reliable estimator of final candidate value. The FOMC
result further separates non-regression from reuse: 20/20 was preserved, yet
the skill was not loaded. Future scheduling should treat target activation,
target property gain, protection non-regression, and protection activation as
separate observations rather than one promotion bit.

\Boundary{No concurrent Quant-$H_0$ sample, repeat, binary-gain event, or sealed
evaluation was included in MT-2. The result supports bounded multi-family
search feasibility, not a benchmark performance claim.}
