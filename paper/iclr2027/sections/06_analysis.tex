\section{Analysis}

The current evidence motivates, but does not yet validate, the proposed method.
This section retains the chronological mechanism evidence, including claims
that were reasonable under earlier file-level blindness checks. The later
six-candidate provenance audit is controlling: whenever an earlier paragraph
describes a candidate-associated gain, its score remains measured but its clean
mechanism or reusable-promotion interpretation is withdrawn if the candidate
projected an optimize-only predicate into a Worker-visible surface.
First, a green free-form probe can be self-confirming: executable relations must
be bound to the public quantity or process semantics. Second, component
registration and local smoke success are insufficient; later trajectories must
show actual activation and the predicted invariant transition. Third, invariant
repair and complete task success are different levels of evidence. A component
may correct a temporal or accounting relation while another unresolved error
keeps the official reward at zero.

AP-2M now establishes a narrow feedback-driven loop result. Round one moved
beyond prompt editing to an executable quantitative audit; round two indexed a
real Worker failure, maintained competing explanations, and refined the audit
after detecting public-clause inconsistencies. This is useful evidence that
runtime experience can alter a later harness decision. It also exposes two
remaining gaps. First, the answer-free probe summary reported
\texttt{missing\_artifact} but omitted the process fact that the Worker spent
its short budget reading and never invoked the component, making direct failure
attribution underidentified. Second, final component-smoke orchestration still
had an early-final branch, repaired after the run but not yet live-verified.
The repair probe also supplied its seed under the data directory while the
Evolver-authored instruction described it as already present at the output
path; the post-run setup now pre-stages the seed consistently.
Neither local audit correctness nor a second Evolver decision shows that the
component changes Worker behavior or official benchmark performance.

The proposed invariant signature may improve search in two ways. It can reduce
the ambiguity of sparse official outcomes, and it can index experience by a
recurring quantitative relation rather than a task identifier. Conditional
routing may also reduce interference by activating temporal, portfolio, or
artifact components only when applicable. These hypotheses remain prospective
until QI-1/QI-2 and matched baselines are measured.

QEC-1 did not show that the proposed certificate representation improves a
strong generic structured Reviewer. Both representations chose the same audits
and reached the same case-level conclusions. The certificate remains a useful
open evidence format, but the current data do not justify making it a mandatory
search or promotion signal.

AP-3 closes a narrower autonomy gap. Starting only from a fresh Quant-$H_0$
trajectory, the Evolver selected a non-prompt component, authored a real
from-scratch probe, consumed its failure, changed the located Research State,
and abstained when the observation did not identify a justified repair. This
is stronger than warm-history AP-2M for bootstrap provenance, but weaker for
benefit: the Worker did not deliver an artifact and no final candidate was
evaluated. Round two also lacked the round-one prediction record, so the run
tested observation-conditioned updating rather than the complete predeclared
prediction-versus-outcome contract.

The bounded intermediate probe verifies the task-visibility repair and
component reach, but not the intended seed-staging intervention. A stale remote
Worker runner left the output empty, so the Worker spent part of the short
probe recovering the seed from its backup before invoking the audit on the
last request. The next valid comparison is therefore the same bounded probe
with the committed runner synchronized. Only if activation is still too late
under that corrected setup should the mechanism reserve a post-audit turn.
This result does not support increasing all Worker budgets or claiming that the
component improves T26.

The paired r2 removes that deployment ambiguity. Once pre-stage was active, the
Worker reached the checker earlier and had two post-audit requests, so terminal
timing is no longer the limiting explanation. The checker reported only legal
temporary join-key warnings and did not distinguish the remaining official
failures; the Worker therefore retained the seed. The next causal test should
change diagnostic coverage through Evolver refinement while holding the blind
Worker probe fixed, rather than adding a general budget increase.

QDR-1 confirms the first half of that prediction. Answer-rich optimize evidence
changed the Evolver's search from a generic artifact audit to an executable
quantitative-consistency component, and a controlled perturbation established
that the component can discriminate the selected mechanism locally. It did not
change Worker behavior. Neither the six- nor ten-iteration blind Worker called
the registered tool; both spent their budgets on broad shell-based inspection
and preserved the 12/17 seed. The second result is important because it rules
out the smallest budget as the sole explanation. The next mechanism should
change when reconciliation is activated or how the Worker selects the
task-conditioned audit, rather than increasing the generic budget again. Until
that transition is observed, local component discrimination must remain
separate from Worker helpfulness and official benchmark gain.

The directed follow-up narrows the activation gap without closing it. The
Evolver twice inferred that a finalization-time audit was too late and authored
a task-conditioned early-call instruction. That intervention changed observed
tool use from zero calls in both QDR-1 probes to one call, but the Worker still
expanded ``bounded inventory'' into five turns of code, paper, and data
inspection. The component then produced actionable errors on turn six, yet the
remaining turn was spent rereading rather than editing. This separates tool
availability from timely Research-State transition and from action on an audit
observation. Another longer prompt or generic budget increase is therefore a
weak next test. A more discriminating comparison is one generic one-shot
checkpoint after a fixed small inventory versus a simplified component call
surface, with first-call position, next action, edit/re-audit, artifact delta,
property delta, and reward measured separately.

P-v2 resolves this mechanism-localization question. The fixed inventory bound
moved the first component call from turn six of seven to turn three of twelve,
and the post-observation reserve allowed the Worker to convert two actionable
findings into an edit, real-data smoke, clean re-audit, and a two-property
official gain. The middleware supplied phase control, not task answers: the
Worker still decided applicability, authored all relation arguments, performed
the repair, and verified the result. Thus component availability was not the
main remaining defect; the prior probe lacked an explicit Research-State
transition and enough causal distance after observation. The next question was
whether the same harness changes a fresh Worker trajectory.

The fresh T26 confirmation closes the next causal gap. Unlike P-v2, no previous
strategy was available, and the Worker activated the component before the
middleware checkpoint could force a decision. The candidate prompt therefore
changed the Worker's construction policy rather than merely enabling a seeded
repair. The audit also had behavioral consequences: the Worker revised the
artifact and reached a clean re-audit before the official net gain. However,
the B7 regression shows that a clean declared-relation certificate is not a
complete correctness certificate. Promotion still needs official properties
and, eventually, repeat or matched-task evidence.

The same run exposes a scaling defect. The Worker declared completion on turn
27, but completion middleware continued requesting redundant transition
smokes through turn 40. Those calls did not establish the causal finding and
inflated cost and latency. This does not invalidate the 15/17 result, but the
terminal condition should stop after artifact delivery, a clean post-edit
audit, and one focused completion check before the method is coordinated over
multiple tasks.

AP-3 r4 does not update the autonomy conclusion. It reached a fresh H0 score
and executed round-one evolution, but a macOS metadata sidecar in the Linux
deploy blocked candidate-history ingestion. The failure was repaired at the
source-transfer boundary and rerun under a new identity; it is neither an
Evolver abstention nor evidence that the proposed search failed.

AP-3 r5 closes the remaining orchestration gap in the bounded protocol. The
Evolver generated a new quant-research skill from the run-local H0 trajectory,
the Worker actually loaded it, and a later Evolver decision used the predicted
versus observed outcome. The calibrated abstention is informative: the probe
showed that passive skill activation did not cause an edit, but it did not test
the competing numerical conventions. The system therefore updated the
intervention-level hypothesis without pretending to have identified the hidden
property cause.

The remaining problem is now more specific than missing feedback plumbing.
MT-1 shows that runtime-conditioned refinement can change executable harness
code, activate it in a fresh Worker, and coincide with an official binary
target gain. However, the short autonomous probes never reached delivery,
whereas the successful confirmation needed 50 Worker turns. Fixed 10--12-call
probes therefore had insufficient causal distance to estimate final candidate
capability.

The protection regression also separates local mechanism value from reusable
harness value. The validator helped the target Worker enforce strict surface
contracts, but its nearby-task run still missed two interior local-vol checks
and a Monte Carlo vanilla-price consistency check. A future candidate must
combine artifact-state validation with a bounded completion/stopping policy
and refine its task applicability from protection evidence. Broad scheduling
remains deferred: evaluating more tasks with the current candidate would scale
cost before resolving its measured scope and stopping defects.

\Boundary{This result is sufficient for a local mechanism-existence claim, but
not for stable transfer or final benchmark performance. A frozen multi-task or

MT-2 narrows the breadth question. The outer loop did not collapse to one
local-vol-specific mutation: independent trajectories produced distinct
temporal-state, calibration, and dependence-integrity components, and one new
target family showed an activated four-property gain. Calibrated abstention on
the underidentified FX residual is also useful; forcing every pair to mutate
would inflate candidate count without improving causal resolution.

The limiting factor is now selection efficiency. Two short probes ended before
delivery but later normal-budget Workers completed and activated their
components, while another short Worker delivered artifacts without using its
tool. A short probe is therefore informative about activation and completion
distance, but is not a reliable estimator of final candidate value. The FOMC
result further separates non-regression from reuse: 20/20 was preserved, yet
the skill was not loaded. Future scheduling should treat target activation,
target property gain, protection non-regression, and protection activation as
separate observations rather than one promotion bit.

\Boundary{No concurrent Quant-$H_0$ sample, repeat, binary-gain event, or sealed
evaluation was included in MT-2. The result supports bounded multi-family
search feasibility, not a benchmark performance claim.}

The point-in-time refinement identifies what a scalable lineage record must
contain. A score and trace are insufficient if the Evolver cannot inspect the
exact component that produced them; the tested parent source, diff, prediction,
and observation must be co-located. First-round breadth discovery and later
within-lineage refinement are also different decision problems. Requiring the
latter to rediscover a cross-task shared mechanism wastes context and can force
an otherwise meaningful feedback update into abstention.

The valid r4 run also demonstrates why activation cannot replace selection.
The refined skill was reachable and used, but the normal Worker neither beat
the parent nor fully produced its predicted label-consistency state. Rollback
was therefore the correct outcome. At scale, a mechanism-specific observed
transition may justify one bounded confirmation when the aggregate score is
noisy, but a plausible prediction alone must not trigger repeated evaluation.
This keeps the parent lineage stable while avoiding all-task evaluation for
clearly unsupported candidates.

The matched State Card canary separates a quant-specific search prior from a
finance-themed prompt. The treatment changed retrieved episodes, selected
relations, component routing, and predeclared observations; the two task
families produced different interventions. Holdings supplied the strongest
evidence: the quant-state candidate reproduced 50/51 despite a large change in
the concurrent Quant-$H_0$ sample and avoided the protection collapse observed
for the generic candidate. However, protection involved a correctly grounded
component skip, not application of the same reconciliation relation. Safety
under inapplicability and cross-task mechanism reuse are therefore different
claims.

Local volatility exposes the next search defect. The quant-state intervention
repaired its selected SVI-admissibility relation but left an orthogonal
forward-variance residual; the broader generic validator initially covered
both but lost full reward on repeat. A single relation is thus a useful search
prior but an unreliable completion certificate. The minimal extension is one
primary relation plus at most one evidence-supported residual-risk relation,
not a closed enumeration of finance failure types.

The timeout also matters for scale. Both long local-vol Workers were labeled
stalled after 20 minutes without new host-side files even while container-side
numerical fitting remained active. One completed naturally; the other reached
its wall-time boundary. Activity, successful completion, and official benefit
must be recorded separately before Main-0. Broad lineage orchestration would
otherwise multiply noisy restarts and expensive long-tail samples without
improving candidate selection.

Search-v2 resolves the earlier completion defect more directly. The generic
Evolver preserved two competing explanations and abstained, while the
quant-state Evolver represented the same evidence as a primary terminal-row
relation plus an orthogonal surface-coverage risk. That representation changed
the intervention decision and produced the first repeated official binary gain
in this QFBench line. The observed terminal values, per-maturity coverage, and
missing-cell scans also connect the score change to the predeclared Research
State correction rather than to finance wording alone.

The matched task reveals the remaining scope problem. The candidate caused the
intended reconciliation behavior and preserved the aggregate score, but it
traded one failed property for another after choosing a looser Monte Carlo
closeness judgment. Aggregate non-regression and property-wise Pareto safety
are therefore different evidence levels. We retain the candidate as a useful
rollback case for lifecycle rehearsal, not as a reusable promoted harness.

The A2 offline replay makes this distinction decision-relevant rather than
terminological. A scalar/count rule cannot distinguish two 38/39 outcomes when
the missing property changes, and would have promoted the local-vol candidate.
The property-wise rule preserves the identity of already-satisfied research
checks and therefore rolls it back, while still accepting the genuinely safe
42/42 holdings case. This is a narrow verification contribution: it says
nothing by itself about whether QRS proposes better candidates.

Before the rehearsal, we froze a public-relation applicability table over the
holdings and local-vol target/protection families. The remaining engineering
gap was a small resumable controller that consumes existing proposal, target,
repeat, and protection reports and commits promotion or rollback. Neither the
controller nor candidate lineage is claimed as the quant-specific novelty.

The thin Main-0 rehearsal closes that controller gap without turning
orchestration into a method claim. The same deterministic lifecycle produced
opposite, appropriate decisions from retained evidence: holdings reached
promotion after repeated property gain and 42/42 protection, while local-vol
rolled back because an aggregate 38/39 tie concealed a failed-property swap.
The latter shows why reward and property count alone are insufficient for a
protection gate; the failed-property set is needed to distinguish aggregate
non-regression from property-wise Pareto safety.

The live holdings child also separates protection safety from relation reuse.
The candidate actually reached its component, but the component recognized
that the Brinson artifact lacked the target holdings schema and returned a
grounded skip. Preserving 42/42 after that call supports scoped
non-interference, not execution of the target reconciliation relation on a
second task. Idempotent resume, zero runtime restart, and complete cleanup show
that retained reports can be consumed without duplicating evaluation or
selection.

\Boundary{This is readiness evidence for the candidate lifecycle substrate.
It does not test whether an Evolver can begin from Quant-$H_0$, propose new
candidates across the planned Main panel, and autonomously reach the same
terminal decisions. The controller is shared experimental infrastructure; the
quant-specific hypothesis remains state- and relation-conditioned component
search. Sealed performance and benchmark-level improvement remain unmeasured.}

Main-0B closes that specific autonomy gap but leaves the scientific selection
problem intact. Two independent Evolver sessions began from Quant-$H_0$,
created executable components, and reached terminal controller decisions with
no experimenter edits. Both achieved a first official binary target gain, yet
only local-vol repeated it and neither candidate was safe enough to promote.
This separates the ability to search for useful interventions from the ability
to evolve a stable Quant Research Worker.

The holdings rollback shows why one successful Worker trajectory is not a
stable capability estimate. The component remained reachable on repeat, but
the full candidate gain disappeared. The local-vol rollback is stronger
mechanism evidence: the audit detected a real aggregate pricing inconsistency,
the Worker acted on it, and the audit later passed, while the official
pointwise surface properties became substantially worse. Activation,
actionability, and an internally green audit therefore cannot substitute for
an observed state-and-outcome relation plus protection.

QPR sharpens that selection problem without pretending to solve statistical
stability. General trajectory stochasticity is already visible in repeated
agent evaluation. The quant-specific issue is its propagation through coupled
continuous states---quotes, forward conventions, fitted surfaces,
differentiated local volatility, Monte Carlo values, and final artifacts---
before a property verifier discretizes the result at tolerance boundaries. A
rotating failed leaf may therefore be more informative than the aggregate
count, while an aggregate tie can still conceal a newly broken public economic
relation.

The canary provides a bounded operational response. Deterministic triage
separated the Main-0B six-property integration regression from the Search-v2
property swap. The answer-free Reviewer preserved that distinction and spent
one additional pair only on the gray case. The repeat did not establish
non-inferiority: the candidate remained 38/39 but moved from a vanilla-
reconciliation failure to barrier reasonableness, while the parent moved from
38/39 to 39/39. The correct terminal action was therefore to stop further
sampling, reject promotion, and retain the component for scope refinement.
This is useful evidence efficiency even though it is not a positive candidate
decision.

The observed Quant-$H_0$ outcomes on this task are \QPRHZeroObserved{}, a
four-property range rather than a variance estimate. The provisional margin of
three served only as a canary rule; a main experiment must predeclare its
non-inferiority margin from additional matched Quant-$H_0$ repetitions. The
QPR-1 hard-negative constraint also prevents an autonomous-discovery claim.

The lineage runway now closes two engineering causes of unnecessary scale
cost. First, benchmark-specific result formats can feed one fixed lifecycle
without forcing their terminal decisions to agree: the QF gray case remains a
hold while the historical QCE property-safe lineage promotes. Second, an
unchanged parent observation need not be sampled again for every candidate.
The live Brinson canary halved completed requests by evaluating only the new
candidate arm. Its token and cost reductions were smaller and are sample-
specific because agent trajectories have variable lengths.

This efficiency evidence must not be confused with search evidence. The QCE
candidate was investigator-seeded, and the QF candidate had already been
retained; the controller selected between supplied observations rather than
discovering a new intervention. The next scientific gate is therefore not
more controller rehearsal. It is a small matched generic-versus-QRS search in
which candidate proposal and evaluation budgets are identical. Within that
run, only a repeated target gain followed by protection evidence accepted by
the fixed controller and an autonomous promotion establishes a stable improved
parent.

The live QCE continuation makes the engineering conclusion stronger while
leaving that scientific boundary unchanged. The controller can now launch a
benchmark-specific child, recover a completed result across restart, avoid
duplicate target work and cost, and continue to an independent repeat. The
same retained relation component was reached in both fresh Workers, and both
beat the 12/17 parent at the property-count level. This is evidence that the
historical QCE replay was not the only supported execution mode.

The repeat also clarifies why relation realization and official correctness
must remain separate evidence levels. Both Workers ended with the same clean
6/6 declared-relation audit and zero information-time residual, yet official
performance moved from 16/17 to 14/17 and the repeat failed two HJ-metric
properties. The component therefore influenced reconciliation consistently
without fully covering the verifier's quantitative semantics or eliminating
Worker stochasticity. Requiring matched protection before promotion is not an
administrative extra: it is the next missing observation needed to distinguish
a useful T26-local intervention from a stable reusable harness.

A0 closes the next matched-search gate, but not in the direction of QRS
superiority. Holdings shows that both search policies can stop when the shared
evidence does not support a reusable cross-task intervention. The QRS arm spent
more proposal work to reach the same abstention, so this family supplies no
efficiency advantage or candidate comparison.

Local volatility is more informative. QRS turned the observed lower-bound SVI
parameters into an explicit admissibility relation and a narrow executable
component. That component changed the Worker's fitted artifacts in the
predicted direction, and QRS reached the first 67/68 target with fewer requests,
tokens, and cost than the generic arm. This is the intended qualitative use of
a Research State Card: it changes evidence retrieval, mechanism localization,
and component routing rather than merely renaming a generic trace.

The terminal evidence nevertheless favors the generic candidate. Its repeat
reached 68/68 and reward one, while QRS remained 67/68 and the selected SVI
correction itself failed to persist even though a different property improved. Both
candidates passed the same 35/39-to-38/39 property-wise protection gate, so the
fixed controller mechanically promoted both. The difference exposes a useful
methodological distinction: a lifecycle promotion can be correct under its
predeclared count-and-protection rule while a stricter scientific comparison
still judges one repeated mechanism footprint less stable.

The result therefore refines rather than confirms the quant-specific story.
State-conditioned relation search can make an intervention interpretable and
may reduce time to a locally useful candidate, but a narrow primary relation is
not a completion certificate. In this sample, the broader generic surface audit
covered the orthogonal forward-variance residual better. The next QRS revision
should improve residual-risk completion or relation composition while
preserving the matched treatment, rather than weakening the generic baseline or
claiming victory from the mechanical promotion bit. In particular, the repeat
did not re-exercise the target's forward-variance failure; the supported
contrast is that generic repaired both failures exposed by its repeat parent,
whereas QRS failed its own selected SVI-admissibility footprint.

The missing persisted State Card and the generic false-zero activation ledger
(raw trace showed two repeat calls and three protection calls) limit automated
mechanism replay. They do not invalidate the
official verifier outcomes, but they prevent a claim that every A0 relation--
activation--outcome link is fully reconstructable from the controller ledger
alone. Future runs retain the State Card separately and bind the exact proposed
component token to child observations.

\Boundary{A0 establishes one bounded, autonomous, repeated, property-safe

A0.1 shows that the next QRS defect is narrower than component availability.
The valid R3 candidate made the admissibility checker reachable, expanded its
executable semantics, and induced ten calls in a fresh Worker. Yet the checker
could only enforce caller-supplied predicates: the Worker checked SVI
\texttt{b} and \texttt{sigma} but omitted the predeclared strict-positivity
relation for \texttt{a}. A green component verdict therefore established
consistency with the submitted predicate set, not completeness of the contract
binding.

This distinction matters for quantitative research artifacts because a task
contract often distributes admissibility across several named columns and
tables. State-conditioned search can identify the right reconciliation locus,
while the Worker can still drop one variable when instantiating the executable
relation. The next bounded search question is whether a general contract-to-
predicate construction or review step improves that binding without embedding
a task answer. Adding more calls to the same checker is not supported by this
run: activation was already abundant.

The negative selection path also behaved as intended once the candidate was
valid. No target gain meant no repeat or protection spend, and the retained
parent was unchanged. R1 and R2 must remain separate engineering observations:
the former was blocked by an unusable State Card interface, while the latter
was an unchanged parent snapshot admitted under an insufficient semantic gate.
Neither is evidence about candidate quality. R3 is the only measured mechanism
candidate, and it provides no official improvement or stability claim.

R4 is the positive counterpart to that diagnosis. Instead of adding another
prompt reminder, the Evolver moved standard-SVI predicate coverage into the
executable checker. This changes the certification contract: a recognized SVI
table auto-binds \texttt{a}, \texttt{b}, and \texttt{sigma}, so an omitted
caller argument can no longer make the profile ignore the level parameter. The
same SVI-\texttt{a} property then resolved on target and independent repeat,
which is stronger evidence than an aggregate score gain whose failed leaf
rotates.

The causal interpretation remains deliberately narrower than the controller's
promotion decision. Both successful Workers reached the checker with already
admissible SVI tables; neither trace showed a violation, a checker-directed
refit, and a repaired second audit. The observed evidence therefore associates
profile availability and activation with repeated SVI-\texttt{a} correction,
but does not prove that the checker alone caused the initial fit to change. A
future controlled perturbation could test that causal edge, but it is not
needed to retain the measured promotion result.

Protection separates two further claims. The candidate was property-safe and
the fresh Worker improved from 38/39 to 39/39, so the lifecycle gate had the
evidence required to promote. Yet all four protection tables were correctly
classified as non-SVI. This supports a small blast radius and absence of false
profile matching, not transfer of the SVI relation to barrier pricing. Keeping
that distinction prevents ordinary fresh-Worker variation from being credited
to an inapplicable quantitative component.

Taken together, R3 and R4 show a useful refinement sequence: runtime evidence
first localized an activated but incomplete contract-to-predicate binding, and
the next Evolver version moved that missing coverage into a reusable executable
profile. The result remains one adaptively selected development family. It is
evidence that lineage refinement can recover from a specific component-
realization gap, not evidence that QRS search is generally superior or that the
promoted harness improves a sealed benchmark panel.

The Final-H0 canary exposes a distinct search-policy issue. QRS reconstructed
the same local-vol SVI admissibility mismatch later repaired in R4, so its
domain representation was informative. Yet the admission contract conflated
three questions: whether the target mechanism was specific and actionable,
whether another task already displayed the same mechanism, and whether history
contained a positive reusable episode. Requiring all three before ACT raised
precision but reduced recall: a reusable fitted-parameter audit was blocked
because the protection task did not itself fit SVI parameters.

This does not imply that protection should be removed. The generic arm shows
why repeat remains necessary: its local-vol target reached 68/68 and reward one,
but repeat kept only the SVI-\texttt{a} correction and lost the forward-variance
footprint. The appropriate separation is therefore target admission versus
promotion. A task-local quantitative relation may warrant one bounded target
evaluation without already appearing in protection; repeat and property-safe
protection should still govern whether it becomes the cumulative incumbent.
Under that interpretation, QRS localization and generic target gain are both
useful development evidence, while neither is a stable final result.

The follow-up gate canary confirms only half of this separation. Relaxing the
cross-task admission requirement recovered the local-vol proposal, showing that
the prior abstention was at least partly a recall problem rather than an
inability to localize or implement the SVI relation. But the same rule also
admitted the holdings control, so the frozen selective ACT/ABSTAIN outcome
failed. A gate that increases recall without an independently credible control
does not yet establish useful selectivity.

The holdings result also cautions against treating experimenter labels as
ground truth. Two independent read-only audits classified it as an invalid
semantic negative control: the Evolver identified a complete weight-state
artifact, counts-derived turnover, self-referential summary checks, and a
successful Brinson contrast, which together define a plausible reconciliation
stress case. The canary therefore does not measure semantic over-admission;
another genuinely unsupported counterexample is required before tuning or
claiming selectivity.

This audit does not rescue the concrete holdings candidate. Its general
reconciliation hypothesis is separable from a task-specific serialization
rule: the public contract permits any unambiguous pair encoding, but the
candidate mandates a two-element array based on answer-rich diagnostics. Such
feedback may guide Evolver diagnosis, but it cannot be embedded as a stricter
Worker-facing requirement when the public contract does not support it. The
candidate should therefore remain unrun and unretained until that convention is
removed. This distinction preserves both findings: the frozen procedural
ACT/ABSTAIN criterion failed, yet the ACT itself is not evidence that the gate
selected an implausible relation.

The local-vol positive case exposes the complementary failure mode: a valid
research diagnosis can still produce an invalid reusable component. Answer-rich
optimization feedback may tell the Evolver that the hidden failure is
\texttt{a==0} and that the verifier expects \texttt{a>0}, but the public task
does not state that predicate. Persisting strict \texttt{a>0} and exact-bound
rejection in a Worker-visible checker, descriptor, and prompt turns permitted
Evolver supervision into leaked Worker supervision. Structural admission and
passing component smokes cannot detect that epistemic provenance error.

Accordingly, the gate canary retains only G+ proposal eligibility, not a valid
candidate. Future construction must translate answer-rich evidence into a
publicly grounded general audit or require an explicit provenance review before
Worker dispatch; it cannot copy a hidden property into the harness. Together
the two audits show why proposal recall, semantic control validity, and
candidate answer-boundary integrity are three separate gates.

The six-candidate retrospective changes the interpretation of the broader
development path. Evaluator isolation cannot be defined only as whether a raw
diagnostic file appears in the Worker context. An Evolver can read
expected-versus-observed feedback, abstract its hidden predicate, and write the
same answer into a general-looking prompt or executable checker. From the blind
Worker's perspective, that semantic projection is supervision by the hidden
verifier even when no score, expected value, or diagnostic text is quoted.

This is particularly easy to miss in quantitative work because predicates such
as strict positivity, terminal completeness, or a weight convention can sound
like domain knowledge. Provenance, not plausibility, is decisive. Raw SVI
\texttt{a>0} may be defensible in some model specifications, but it cannot be
introduced as a required task predicate when the public contract does not state
it and the exact failing property came from answer-rich feedback. Likewise, a
general reconciliation invariant does not license an unstated turnover formula
or a pair-array convention that contradicts the public allowance.

The required gate must therefore inspect semantic diffs, not only files or
component roles. Before Worker dispatch, every decision-changing requirement
in prompts, tool code, descriptions, validators, skills, memory, middleware,
and routing must cite public evidence or be removed. Historical contaminated
episodes must be excluded from positive retrieval so they cannot propagate the
same predicate into later candidates. The next valid evidence unit is not
another repair of these six candidates, but one fresh public-only proposal that
passes provenance review before a blind Worker run and then obtains a retained
official gain.

The Candidate Information-Set Reviewer canary provides the first bounded
evidence that this provenance question can be delegated without giving the
Reviewer mutation or promotion authority. On the same mixed fixture, it
rejected three plausible quantitative rules whose decisive support was
optimize-only while preserving two neighboring rules directly stated by the
public tasks. The coverage pass matters separately: a correct verdict over an
incomplete claim inventory would still allow an undeclared semantic projection
to reach the Worker.

One response does not establish a reliable universal gate. The current result
measures schema adherence and directional discrimination on five predeclared
claims, not robustness to paraphrase, omitted claims, longer diffs, interacting
components, or adversarial rationalization. The Reviewer must therefore sit
inside the deterministic controller boundary: every candidate is reviewed
after mutation and before Worker dispatch, overall \texttt{REJECT} stops the
lineage, and the Reviewer can never promote. Only a fresh public-only candidate
that passes this integrated gate and then improves an official blind-Worker
target can change readiness from \texttt{NO-GO}.

The FX prescreen also separates transport/accounting success from a valid
research trajectory. Three requests completed and the final request consumed
32,000 output tokens, yet the Worker runtime exposed neither content nor tool
