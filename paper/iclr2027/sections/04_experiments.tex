\section{Experimental Design}

We organize the prospective study around four questions.

\begin{description}
    \item[RQ1: QRS main.] Can a QRS-only cumulative lineage produce
    information-eligible, repeat-consistent, protection-safe harness
    promotions and improve a frozen no-feedback sealed outcome vector relative
    to Quant-H0-S6?
    \item[RQ2: Quant-specific mechanism.] Holding the controller, public
    evidence, tasks, model, and budget fixed, how does removing State-Card
    construction and state-conditioned retrieval/routing change proposal,
    Review, state-transition, and promotion outcomes?
    \item[RQ3: External baseline.] Under common public task information,
    Worker model, sealed panel, and outer resource budget, how does QRS compare
    with an independently reproduced AHE baseline?
    \item[RQ4: Efficiency.] What requests, tokens, verifier calls, cost, and
    wall time are required to reach the first stable QRS promotion and the
    frozen sealed endpoints?
\end{description}

\subsection{Three Separate Experiment Tracks}

The headline QFBench experiment is QRS-only. Independent cumulative QRS
lineages start from fresh Quant-H0-S6 instances, maintain their own incumbent,
and traverse a predeclared family order. Quant-H0-S6 retains the shell interface
but emits concise S1--S6 stage summaries; it is a new baseline and is never
mixed with historical Quant-H0 observations.

The quant-specific mechanism study is a separately frozen QRS-no-State
executable. It removes the State Card and state-conditioned retrieval/routing
while keeping Quant-H0-S6, public evidence, task order, model, budget,
Candidate Review, and downstream lifecycle fixed. Runs share no candidates or
post-treatment history. This ablation is not called Generic or AHE and is not a
runtime switch in the final QRS release.

AHE is reproduced independently from a pinned upstream implementation. We
first establish source-faithful execution, then add the minimum adapter needed
to consume the same quantitative public tasks. A common Candidate
Information-Set Review is applied after proposal and before the blind Worker as
an experimental-integrity gate, not as an AHE search component. The AHE code
and lifecycle remain outside the released QRS implementation.

The statistical unit is an independent cumulative lineage, not an individual
task inside it. Families in a lineage are dependent because later candidates
inherit earlier promoted changes. Paper-level inference requires multiple
independent lineages with counterbalanced family order; a small canary is
reported as a descriptive mechanism study without a superiority claim.

\subsection{Task-Pool Disclosure and Evidence Splits}

Before the first QRS proposal, we freeze a manifest containing the full
candidate pool, public inclusion and exclusion criteria, family strata, the
person-independent selection rule within each stratum, selected
target/repeat/protection roles, all exclusions and reasons, and the freeze
time. Purposeful family selection is allowed: a development task may require a
valid prescribed runtime, a publicly auditable contract, nontrivial fresh
Quant-H0-S6 headroom, and an answer-free protection task. We identify this as a
deliberately selected mechanism-development panel rather than an unselected
sample of QFBench. The selection is disclosed rather than hidden.

Any task, trajectory, or outcome that affects candidate generation,
refinement, promotion, or task selection is development evidence. A separate
family-stratified sealed panel is frozen before optimization from tasks that did
not influence those decisions. Each frozen Quant-H0-S6, QRS, and external AHE
endpoint sees each sealed task once; sealed outcomes do not return to the
Evolver, Candidate Reviewer, retrieval index, promotion controller, or task
selector. QuantCodeEval is a later cross-benchmark check and does not block the
first QFBench QRS campaign.

\subsection{Candidate Information Boundary and Promotion}

Quant-H0-S6 is the only unevolved Worker that may run without Candidate
Review. Every changed candidate must fail closed unless a trusted controller
has reviewed the exact cumulative Quant-H0-S6-to-effective-candidate material,
including any Worker directive or runtime overlay, and recorded overall
\texttt{PASS} with complete claim coverage. Public support is read from frozen
task contracts or predeclared references. Optimize-only diagnostics and Review
feedback cannot support a Worker-visible claim and do not enter the Worker.
The Candidate Reviewer evaluates the candidate information set; it does not
review the Worker answer and cannot mutate, run, retain, or promote a candidate.

Each predeclared family-round permits one initial proposal and at most one
evidence-grounded refinement. A Review-eligible target must strictly improve
the official outcome without introducing a failed property and must resolve a
nonempty property footprint. An independent repeat must reproduce that
footprint without a new failure. The answer-free protection candidate must be
aggregate-safe and its failed-property set must be a subset of the parent set.
Only then does the controller update the official incumbent. Invalid runtime
observations may receive only one separately frozen setup recovery; tasks,
routes, and budgets are never adaptively replaced.

\subsection{Primary Endpoints}

The QRS development endpoint is stable-promotion rate
\begin{equation}
    \operatorname{SPR}_{\mathrm{QRS}}
    = \frac{\sum_{\ell,f} \mathbb{1}[\text{Review pass, target gain,
    repeat-consistent, protection-safe}]}{N_{\mathrm{predeclared\ QRS\ opportunities}}}.
\end{equation}
The conservative denominator includes every predeclared opportunity.
\texttt{ABSTAIN}, rejected admission, Review \texttt{REJECT} or
\texttt{INCONCLUSIVE}, no-gain target, repeat inconsistency, unsafe protection,
and an execution that remains invalid after its frozen recovery all count as
zero. A separately labelled execution-valid sensitivity analysis may omit only
the last category. Neither analysis substitutes another task or unreported
best-of-$N$ attempt.

The headline final endpoint is the complete sealed official task and property
vector for frozen Quant-H0-S6 and the frozen QRS incumbent. We report every
task, an equal-weight task mean inside each family, and an equal-weight macro
across families. The macro never replaces the per-task vector.

The QRS-no-State study uses the same lifecycle, so proposal, Review,
state-transition, promotion, sealed-vector, and cost funnels are directly
comparable across separate runs. AHE may retain a distinct internal lifecycle;
the defensible cross-method endpoint is therefore its common sealed official
vector and resource use under the same public inputs, Worker model, task panel,
and outer budget. We do not silently relabel a generic QRS mode as AHE or force
AHE into QRS-specific promotion semantics.

\subsection{Diagnostics, Efficiency, and Reporting}

The proposal, admission, Candidate Review, exposure or activation, Research
State correction, official target gain, repeat consistency, and protection
safety rates form a diagnostic funnel. These explain the primary endpoints and
are not individually called benchmark wins. We additionally report total model
requests, tokens, provider cost, verifier calls, and wall time per lineage and
to the first stable promotion. A lineage without a promotion is charged its
full frozen budget. Statistical comparisons, if sample size permits, operate on
independent-lineage outcomes; otherwise the paper reports descriptive vectors
and intervals without a significance or superiority claim.

\Boundary{The universal candidate-dispatch gate, Quant-H0-S6 canary, QRS main
manifest, independent no-State ablation, source-faithful AHE reproduction, and
sealed panel remain prospective. Main launch is blocked until one fresh
public-only QRS candidate passes Candidate Information-Set Review, reaches a
blind Worker as the exact reviewed harness, and produces a retained strict
official gain. Repeat and protection are additionally required before calling
that candidate stable.}
