\section{Experimental Design}

We organize the prospective study around four questions.

\begin{description}
    \item[RQ1: QRS main.] Can a QRS-only cumulative lineage produce
    information-eligible, repeated focus-family gains with fixed cross-family
    task-reward sentinels, and improve a frozen within-QFBench feedback-sealed
    matched outcome vector relative
    to the imported frozen Primitive-H0?
    \item[RQ2: Quant-specific mechanism.] Holding the controller, public
    evidence, tasks, model, and budget fixed, how does removing State-Card
    construction and state-conditioned retrieval/routing change proposal,
    Review, state-transition, and promotion outcomes?
    \item[RQ3: External baseline.] Under common public task information,
    Worker model, within-QFBench feedback-sealed matched panel, and outer
    resource budget, how does QRS compare with an independently reproduced AHE
    baseline?
    \item[RQ4: Efficiency.] What requests, tokens, verifier calls, cost, and
    wall time are required to reach the first stable QRS promotion and the
    frozen feedback-sealed matched endpoints?
\end{description}

\subsection{Three Separate Experiment Tracks}

The headline QFBench experiment is QRS-only. One primary cumulative QRS
lineage starts from fresh executions of one imported frozen Primitive-H0,
maintains one incumbent, and traverses a predeclared family order. A second
counterbalanced lineage is a later robustness experiment, not part of the
primary launch.
Primitive-H0 is the reference implementation of a standard base-harness
adapter: an independently authored harness may be explored outside QRS and then
enter the same scheduler after it materializes the required Worker layout,
public information boundary, passive S1--S6 interface, enumerable mutation
surfaces, and fixed runtime. The scheduler does not optimize or modify that
base in place.

Before main, a three-arm construct canary compares legacy Quant-H0,
Quant-H0-S6-Core, and S6-Full. Core isolates state observability; Full adds
detailed human-authored stage advice and is reported as a strong workflow
baseline rather than treated as learned QRS capability. A genuine Core or Full
gain is disclosed and triggers harder development-task screening; neither
baseline is weakened merely to manufacture evolution headroom.

The quant-specific mechanism study is a separately frozen QRS-no-State
executable. It removes the State Card and state-conditioned retrieval/routing
while keeping Primitive-H0, public evidence, task order, model, budget,
Candidate Review, and downstream lifecycle fixed. Runs share no candidates or
post-treatment history. This ablation is not called Generic or AHE and is not a
runtime switch in the final QRS release.

AHE is reproduced independently from a pinned upstream implementation. We
first establish source-faithful execution, then add the minimum adapter needed
to consume the same quantitative public tasks. A common Candidate
Information-Set Review is applied after proposal and before the blind Worker as
an information-admissibility and anti-leak gate, not as an AHE search component
or utility judge. The AHE code and lifecycle remain outside the released QRS
implementation.

Families and repeated task cells in the lineage are dependent because later
candidates inherit earlier promoted changes. The primary run therefore reports
complete vectors and descriptive paired effects without treating task cells as
independent samples or making a significance claim.

\subsection{Task-Pool Disclosure and Evidence Splits}

Before the first QRS proposal, we freeze a public-metadata partition. We retain
upstream binary tasks, exclude three disclosed construct-calibration tasks,
select two feedback-sealed tasks per family using a deterministic metadata-only
draw, and place every remaining eligible task into development. Thus the
45-task development bank is the complete remainder rather than a
score-stratified or researcher-picked subset. Primitive-H0 exploratory profile
selection is a separate pre-scheduler initialization step and does not choose
or prune development tasks.

Any task, trajectory, or outcome that affects candidate generation,
refinement, promotion, or task selection is development evidence. A separate
family-stratified feedback-sealed panel is frozen before optimization from
tasks that did not influence those decisions. Each frozen Primitive-H0, QRS,
and external AHE
endpoint sees each feedback-sealed task in two fresh repetitions;
feedback-sealed outcomes do not return to the
Evolver, Candidate Reviewer, retrieval index, promotion controller, or task
selector. QuantCodeEval is a later cross-benchmark check and does not block the
first QFBench QRS campaign.

\subsection{Candidate Information Boundary and Promotion}

The imported frozen Primitive-H0 is the only unevolved Worker that may run without Candidate
Review. Every changed candidate must fail closed unless a trusted controller
has reviewed the exact cumulative frozen-$H_0$-to-effective-candidate material,
including any Worker directive or runtime overlay, and recorded overall
\texttt{PASS} with complete claim coverage. Public support is read from frozen
task contracts or predeclared references. A task-specific numeric or output
predicate requires frozen public task-contract support. A task-agnostic harness
policy may instead use the frozen framework reference plus exact controller-
trusted answer-free development observations actually accessed by the Evolver.
Optimize-only diagnostics, expected values, checker output, failed properties,
official outcomes, and Review feedback cannot support a Worker-visible claim
and do not enter the Worker. The Candidate Reviewer evaluates information-set
admissibility and coverage; it does not review the Worker answer, judge utility,
or mutate, run, retain, or promote a candidate. Review \texttt{PASS} means only
that the exact candidate is eligible for the blind matched gate.

Each predeclared family round permits exactly one global-workflow proposal and
no score-driven refinement. On Review \texttt{PASS}, the exact parent and
reviewed candidate run twice on the complete focus family plus one fixed
development anchor from each of the other five families, with arm order
reversed. The controller promotes only when all cells are valid and
protocol-complete, every focus and anchor task is task-reward non-regressing in
both repetitions, the focus-family mean strictly improves twice, and at least
one identical focus task wins twice. Anchor wins cannot satisfy the focus-gain
condition. This is task-reward protection, not a failed-property-set claim.
The fixed other-family anchors are deterministic sentinels for a bounded
cross-family regression check; they do not establish development-wide
protection.
Legal \texttt{ABSTAIN}, Review non-PASS, tie-only gain, unstable gain, or valid
regression retains the incumbent and continues to the next family. Invalid
runtime observations may receive only one separately frozen setup recovery;
tasks, routes, and budgets are never adaptively replaced.

\subsection{Primary Endpoints}

The descriptive QRS development funnel includes the panel-promotion fraction
\begin{equation}
    \operatorname{PPR}_{\mathrm{QRS}}
    = \frac{\sum_f \mathbb{1}[\text{Review pass, repeated focus gain,
    focus/anchor task-reward non-regression}]}{6}.
\end{equation}
The conservative denominator includes every predeclared opportunity.
\texttt{ABSTAIN}, rejected admission, Review \texttt{REJECT} or
\texttt{INCONCLUSIVE}, no repeated focus gain, focus/anchor regression,
and an execution that remains invalid after its frozen recovery all count as
zero. A separately labelled execution-valid sensitivity analysis may omit only
the last category. Neither analysis substitutes another task or unreported
best-of-$N$ attempt.
Because family panels differ in task count and occur in an ordered cumulative
lineage, $x/6$ is a descriptive funnel fraction, not an estimator over
exchangeable or independent trials.

The headline final endpoint is the complete within-QFBench feedback-sealed
matched official task vector for two fresh executions of the frozen
Primitive-H0 and retained QRS incumbent. We
report 24 repetition-level paired W/T/L outcomes, 12 task-level mean W/T/L
outcomes, every task vector, and family rows. Because the panel contains exactly
two tasks per family, the equal-task mean and equal-family macro are
numerically identical by design and are not presented as independent evidence.
Passed/total values are secondary diagnostics when complete; normalized
headroom closure is appendix-only descriptive context.

The QRS-no-State study uses the same lifecycle, so proposal, Review,
state-transition, promotion, feedback-sealed-vector, and cost funnels are
directly comparable across separate runs. AHE may retain a distinct internal
lifecycle;
the defensible cross-method endpoint is therefore its common within-QFBench
feedback-sealed matched official vector and resource use under the same public
inputs, Worker model, task panel,
and outer budget. We do not silently relabel a generic QRS mode as AHE or force
AHE into QRS-specific promotion semantics. The claim is limited to
within-QFBench feedback-sealed matched transfer.

\subsection{Diagnostics, Efficiency, and Reporting}

The proposal, admission, Candidate Review, exposure or activation, Research
State correction, official target gain, repeat consistency, and protection
safety rates form a diagnostic funnel. These explain the primary endpoints and
are not individually called benchmark wins. We additionally report total model
requests, tokens, provider cost, verifier calls, and wall time per lineage and
to the first stable promotion. A lineage without a promotion is charged its
full frozen budget. Statistical comparisons, if sample size permits, operate on
independent-lineage outcomes; otherwise the paper reports descriptive vectors
and intervals without a significance or superiority claim.

\Boundary{The candidate-dispatch gate, Primitive-H0 handoff, trajectory bank,
dynamic-incumbent scheduler, answer-free panel history, mutation boundary, and
launch materializer are implemented locally. The Primitive protocol canary has
passed 3/3 valid strict-telemetry cells. Retained mini runs R1--R3 did not
complete the positive Review path: R1 was setup-invalid, R2 ended in a legal
fail-closed abstention under an oversized and compaction-corrupted evidence
view, and R3 received a genuine but non-PASS Review. The main run, no-State
ablation, source-faithful AHE reproduction, and feedback-sealed panel remain
prospective. Main launch is blocked until one fresh policy-corrected candidate
passes Candidate Information-Set Review, reaches 12 matched cells as the exact
reviewed snapshot, hands off answer-free history, and cleanly resumes. Its
matched terminal may be \texttt{PROMOTE} or a scientifically valid
\texttt{RETAIN}/\texttt{ROLLBACK}; retained utility gain is a Main outcome, not
an engineering-readiness requirement.}
