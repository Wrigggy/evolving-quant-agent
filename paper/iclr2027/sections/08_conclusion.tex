\section{Conclusion}

We formulate quantitative-agent harness evolution as intervention on an open,
task-conditioned Research State process. The Evolver connects expected and
observed Worker states to a selected harness component and a falsifiable target
transition, using executable quantitative invariants as observations when
applicable. A mandatory candidate-focused Information-Set Reviewer audits the
complete cumulative changed harness and the provenance of every
decision-changing claim before a blind Worker can receive it. The Reviewer
reviews the candidate information set, not the Worker answer, and has no
mutation or promotion authority. Rather than equating harness improvement
with a single final answer, the evaluation separates information-set
eligibility, exposure or activation, state correction, official target gain,
repeated focus-family promotion under fixed cross-family task-reward sentinels,
and sealed performance. The complete metadata-defined development remainder is
disclosed, and the final endpoint is reserved for a frozen no-feedback panel. A
standard frozen-base adapter separates
initial harness construction from the QRS loop: any contract-compatible base
harness may be authored externally, materialized and frozen, after which the
same trajectory-bank, Review, promotion and sealed scheduler applies. The
headline experiment is QRS-only; a
QRS-no-State mechanism ablation and a source-faithful AHE reproduction remain
separate experiment tracks and separate implementations. \Pending{Conclude
with the QRS repeated panel-promotion and sealed official vectors after the
live exact-candidate gate and prospective campaign are complete.}
