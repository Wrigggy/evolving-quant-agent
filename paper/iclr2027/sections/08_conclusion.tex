\section{Conclusion}

We formulate quantitative-agent harness evolution as intervention on an open,
task-conditioned Research State process. The Evolver connects expected and
observed Worker states to a selected harness component and a falsifiable target
transition, using executable quantitative invariants as observations when
applicable. A mandatory arm-blind Candidate Information-Set Reviewer audits the
complete changed harness and the provenance of every decision-changing claim
before a blind Worker can receive it. Rather than equating harness improvement
with a single final answer, the evaluation separates information-set
eligibility, exposure or activation, state correction, official target gain,
stable protection-safe promotion, and sealed performance. Purposefully selected
development families are disclosed as such, and the final endpoint is reserved
for a frozen no-feedback panel. \Pending{Conclude with the stable-promotion and
sealed official vectors after the prospective matched campaigns are complete.}
