\section{Limitations}

Quantitative invariants are not complete specifications. A strategy can satisfy
temporal, accounting, and artifact checks while remaining economically useless
or failing another official property. Conversely, an invariant may be
inapplicable or underspecified by the public task. We therefore retain \NA{} and
\Unknown{} outcomes and keep official evaluation separate.

Candidate Information-Set Review is also not a proof system. Public contracts
can be ambiguous, a Reviewer can miss an undeclared exposure, and a supported
rule can still be useless. We therefore treat \texttt{PASS} only as permission
for a blind Worker evaluation, while \texttt{INCONCLUSIVE} blocks exposure
rather than being coerced into acceptance. The retrospective contamination of
six development candidates shows why semantic claim provenance, rather than
raw-file isolation alone, is necessary; it also means those historical scores
cannot validate the final method.

The current implementation has not yet made Candidate Review a universal
dispatch invariant. Legacy and preconstructed-candidate paths, post-admission
Worker directives, optional cumulative baselines, and plan-supplied public
excerpts remain concrete gaps. The public-only holdings line validates the
non-PASS stop, but no real public-only PASS candidate has yet crossed the exact
reviewed-harness boundary to a blind Worker. Quant-H0-S6 is likewise a proposed
new baseline: imposing observable stage checkpoints can itself change Worker
capability, token use, and tool sequencing, so it requires fresh parents and
cannot be evaluated by mixing historical Quant-H0 scores.

Repeated adaptation to optimization tasks creates selection overfitting; no
task that influences candidate selection is described as out of sample. The
development task panel is deliberately family-stratified for mechanism
observability and may not represent the full QFBench distribution. We disclose
the eligible pool, selection rule, and exclusions rather than hiding this human
design choice. Only a separately frozen no-feedback panel can support the final
generalization endpoint. With few independent cumulative lineages, results are
descriptive and cannot support a broad significance or superiority claim. The
current method does not train model weights and does not establish live-market
alpha, universal quantitative-research capability, or fully autonomous
experiment scheduling. Component interactions may also make individually useful
edits non-additive.
