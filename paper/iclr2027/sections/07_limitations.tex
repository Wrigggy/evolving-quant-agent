\section{Limitations}

Quantitative invariants are not complete specifications. A strategy can satisfy
temporal, accounting, and artifact checks while remaining economically useless
or failing another official property. Conversely, an invariant may be
inapplicable or underspecified by the public task. We therefore retain \NA{} and
\Unknown{} outcomes and keep official evaluation separate.

Candidate Information-Set Review is also not a proof system. Public contracts
can be ambiguous, a Reviewer can miss an undeclared exposure, and a supported
rule can still be useless. We therefore treat \texttt{PASS} only as permission
for a blind Worker evaluation, while \texttt{INCONCLUSIVE} blocks exposure
rather than being coerced into acceptance. The retrospective contamination of
six development candidates shows why semantic claim provenance, rather than
raw-file isolation alone, is necessary; it also means those historical scores
cannot validate the final method.

The exact-candidate dispatch boundary is implemented locally: changed and
preconstructed candidates fail closed without Review, cumulative material is
constructed from the frozen base, a passing candidate is copied to a
run-scoped reviewed snapshot, and the Worker is bound to that snapshot. This
still is not empirical proof of the positive path. The public-only holdings
line validates non-PASS stopping, but no real public-only PASS candidate has
yet crossed the exact reviewed-harness boundary to a blind Worker. The bounded
mini-scheduler canary is therefore a launch gate rather than a claimed result.
Primitive-H0 has passed a three-task structured-interface gate, but it remains
a prospective matched baseline: imposing observable stage checkpoints can
itself change Worker capability, token use, and tool sequencing, so capability
requires fresh matched executions and cannot be evaluated by mixing historical
Quant-H0 scores. The base enters through a standard frozen-harness
adapter. This modularity permits different independently authored initial
harnesses, but the resulting QRS estimate is conditional on the imported base;
it does not establish invariance to initialization. The separately reported
Core and S6-Full baselines contain stronger human-authored process advice; any
gain from them is not QRS evolution.

Repeated adaptation to development tasks creates selection overfitting; no
task that influences candidate selection is described as out of sample. The
development bank is the complete eligible metadata-defined remainder after
three disclosed construct tasks and a deterministic two-per-family sealed
partition are removed. This avoids score-conditioned task pruning, but neither
the resulting 45-task bank nor its five cross-family anchors necessarily
represent every quantitative research distribution. Only the separately
frozen no-feedback panel can support the final generalization endpoint. The
primary experiment contains one cumulative lineage; its family opportunities
and task repetitions are dependent, so results are descriptive and cannot
support a broad significance or superiority claim. Promotion is protected at
the native task-reward level, not by failed-property-set inclusion; rotating
subproperty failures can therefore remain hidden beneath a task-level tie. The
current method does not train model weights and does not establish live-market
alpha, universal quantitative-research capability, or open-ended autonomous
experiment scheduling. Component interactions may also make individually
useful edits non-additive.
