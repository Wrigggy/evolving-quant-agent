# QFBench independent-pair breadth result

Date: 2026-08-21

## Outcome

The fixed multi-task search mechanism has now run over four independent
QFBench task pairs from the same Quant-$H_0$ backbone. The Evolvers shared no
new candidates with one another. Three returned `ACT` and produced executable
multi-file candidates; one returned a calibrated `ABSTAIN`. Normal-budget
fresh Workers subsequently activated two different autonomous components on
two different target families.

One pair produced a property gain. The point-in-time candidate added an
`effective-state-reconciliation` skill. A fresh `13f-amendment-aware-crowding`
Worker loaded that skill and scored 50/51, compared with the corrected retained
Quant-$H_0$ trajectory at 46/51. The official binary reward remained zero. The
same frozen candidate preserved `fomc-tone-event-study` at 20/20, but that
Worker did not load the skill. This is target-helpful and protection-safe
evidence, not observed cross-task component reuse.

The copula candidate added an executable artifact validator and a support-
boundary rule for negative Kendall tau. Its normal-budget Worker called the
validator and completed all five deliverables, but remained 27/28, tied with
the corrected retained comparator. The option-surface candidate was not called
by its short Worker and regressed from 67/68 to 65/68. The carry/basis Evolver
abstained because answer-free evidence could not distinguish three competing
causes of the target's single failure.

## Pair outcomes

| Pair | Evolver | Component use and official outcome | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| FX forward + crypto basis | ABSTAIN | No candidate or Worker | Calibrated non-match |
| 13F + FOMC | ACT | Target activated, 46/51 to 50/51; protection stayed 20/20 without activation | Retain as bounded positive |
| Dupire + variance swap | ACT | Tool not called; target 67/68 to 65/68 | Stop lineage |
| Equity copula + copula sampling | ACT | Normal-budget target activated; 27/28 to 27/28 | Retain as activation/tie |

The batch used 152 completed model requests, 8,282,822 tokens, and
$0.355535672. A first attempt to start the normal-budget 13F confirmation used
the wrong CLI shape for `--arm` and failed before any model request; a new run
ID with `candidate=PATH` completed normally.

## What the batch verifies

Measured:

- the same outer loop can produce different finance-specific components for
  temporal holdings state, option calibration, and copula numeric integrity;
- independent Evolvers can choose both `ACT` and evidence-grounded `ABSTAIN`;
- short probes often measure completion distance rather than final harness
  capability: both 13F and copula short probes produced no artifact, while
  their normal-budget Workers completed and activated the component;
- an autonomously synthesized component was activated on a fresh task family
  and coincided with a four-property gain; and
- a protection task can remain at full reward under the frozen candidate.

Not measured:

- there was no binary reward gain in this batch;
- there was no concurrent H0 sample, so the 46-to-50 comparison is a retained-
  trajectory gain rather than a paired causal estimate;
- the protection Worker did not load the skill, so component transfer is not
  established;
- no repeat, sealed test set, or benchmark-wide scheduler was run; and
- the experimenter arranged the normal-budget confirmations after the short
  probes, so this is not yet a fully autonomous cost-aware search policy.

## Decision

Retain the point-in-time candidate and all negative lineages. The next bounded
mechanism problem is no longer whether the Evolver can search more than one
quant task family. It can. The remaining problems are search efficiency,
component activation on matched protection tasks, and candidate selection
under Worker stochasticity. Do not expand to a full benchmark scheduler until
those three issues have a small fixed-policy test.
