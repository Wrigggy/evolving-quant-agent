## Analysis

R4 isolates a clean distinction between claim coverage and evidential support.
The Evolver used the R3 review and made its claims coextensive with the
candidate prompt: the two previously undeclared operations were now explicitly
covered. Yet it preserved the same two normative procedure families and tried
to justify them through public clauses plus benchmark-independent principles.
The Reviewer correctly withheld support because the single permitted public
instruction did not directly entail the full rules.

This is stronger than saying refinement merely ignored feedback. The feedback
changed the proposal and repaired the measured coverage defect, but did not
produce an eligible candidate. It also shows why redeclaring a disputed rule is
not equivalent to removing it: a complete inventory permits the support audit
to run cleanly, while the semantic verdict can remain `INCONCLUSIVE`.

A third refinement would repeat the same evidence boundary without a new
information source or a materially different intervention. The retained R2,
R3, and R4 sequence is therefore sufficient to terminate this holdings lineage
at c3. That stopping decision is methodological economy, not evidence that the
prompt is harmful or that another independently designed holdings intervention
could not work.

## Interpretation boundary

R4 measures use of prior Reviewer evidence, proposal construction, candidate
admission, cumulative trusted-package construction, repaired claim coverage,
claim-level public-support classification, controller hold, dual accounting,
terminal idempotence, and cleanup. It does not measure Worker uptake, task
score, process gain, official benchmark improvement, stable reuse, transfer,
or main-experiment readiness. No benchmark-gain claim is available because no
Worker or verifier ran.

## Final lineage disposition

Terminate the public-only holdings refinement lineage. Do not construct c4,
and do not send c3 to a Worker. A future holdings study would require a newly
motivated, separately frozen intervention with a materially different evidence
or mechanism premise; it would not be a continuation authorized by this
lineage.
