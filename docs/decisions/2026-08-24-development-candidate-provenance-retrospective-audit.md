# Development-candidate provenance retrospective audit

Date: 2026-08-24
Status: retained superseding audit; 6/6 contaminated; main-experiment readiness `NO-GO`

## Decision

Downgrade the scientific interpretation of all six evaluated development
candidates in Search-v2, A0, R4, and Final-H0. Their official scores, rewards,
property vectors, activation records, costs, and controller decisions remain
measured historical facts. None of the six candidates remains valid evidence of
a harness improvement, scientific promotion, stable reuse, or search-method
gain because each projected a hidden expected predicate or verifier-specific
convention from Evolver-only answer-rich diagnostics into a Worker-visible
candidate.

The raw diagnostic files were not directly supplied to Workers. That isolation
was real but insufficient. In each contaminated lineage, the Evolver translated
the diagnostic's semantic answer into a prompt rule, tool description,
executable check, activation instruction, or repair requirement. The relevant
research-integrity boundary is therefore semantic-predicate provenance, not
only raw-file visibility.

Main-experiment readiness is `NO-GO` until two conditions hold: a universal
pre-Worker provenance gate covers every mutable component role, and at least
one fresh public-only target passes that gate and produces a retained official
improvement with a blind Worker.

## Audit rule

A candidate is `CONTAMINATED` when all three conditions hold:

1. the candidate requires a predicate or convention not grounded in the public
   task;
2. that requirement matches an expected-versus-observed fact available in an
   Evolver-only optimization diagnostic; and
3. the requirement enters a reusable Worker-visible harness surface or directs
   the Worker to repair toward the hidden expectation.

`CLEAN` requires affirmative public provenance for every decision-changing
semantic predicate. `INCONCLUSIVE` is reserved for cases where the provenance
chain cannot be reconstructed. The six audited chains were reconstructable:
0 clean, 6 contaminated, 0 inconclusive.

## Candidate findings

### 1. Search-v2 local-vol prompt candidate — `CONTAMINATED`

The optimize-only diagnostic stated that the terminal maturity row was missing
`local_vol_atm` and that the expected value was finite and positive at every
maturity. The prompt-only candidate then required every terminal maturity cell
to be finite and positive and prescribed a one-sided terminal derivative. The
public instruction did not ground that exact terminal-row rule.

Retain target 65/68 to 68/68 and repeat 66/68 to 68/68 as official observations,
plus the 38/39 to 38/39 matched-task property swap. Withdraw the valid repeated
harness-gain and Search-v2 mechanism-gain interpretation.

### 2. A0 generic local-vol c1 — `CONTAMINATED`

The public task did not state raw SVI `a>0`. The candidate encoded strict
`a>0`, numerical lower and bound-saturation thresholds, and instructions to
refit until the hidden predicate passed across prompt, descriptor, and
executable audit code.

Retain target 66/68 to 67/68, repeat 66/68 to 68/68, and protection 35/39 to
38/39 as measured outcomes. Withdraw the prior scientific promotion and
repeated valid harness-improvement interpretation.

### 3. A0 QRS local-vol c1 — `CONTAMINATED`

The answer-rich diagnostic exposed the boundary-zero SVI intercept failure.
The candidate taught the blind Worker that an intercept exactly equal to zero
violated the output contract, instructed a strictly positive refit, and
activated a checker on the corresponding raw column. That semantic predicate
was not public.

Retain target and repeat counts and the observed component calls. Withdraw the
relation-aligned target change as valid QRS mechanism evidence.

### 4. R4 standard-SVI c3 — `CONTAMINATED`

R4 used the Evolver-only `test_svi_a_positive` failure and the R3 omission of
`a` to create a signature-gated standard-SVI profile that automatically and
non-weakenably enforced `a>0`. Moving the predicate from caller choice into a
reusable executable profile made the semantic projection stronger, not clean.

Retain the two 67/68 to 68/68 outcomes, protection 38/39 to 39/39, and recorded
controller `PROMOTE`. Withdraw valid promotion, repeated mechanism gain, and
reuse of c3.

### 5. Final-H0 generic local-vol c2 — `CONTAMINATED`

The candidate encoded `a>0` in its tool, descriptor, and prompt, including an
instruction to apply the convention even when task text did not spell it out.
The diagnostic-only `a==0`/`a>0` predicate therefore reached the blind Worker.

Retain target 66/68 to 68/68 and repeat 66/68 to 67/68 as official contaminated-
development observations. Withdraw candidate-associated binary gain.

### 6. Final-H0 generic holdings c1 — `CONTAMINATED`

The general final-state reconciliation idea was plausible, but the exact
candidate bundled it with answer-derived conventions. The public task did not
state the weight-based turnover formula and explicitly allowed any unambiguous
pair encoding; the candidate required the reference weight convention and a
two-element pair structure.

Retain target 46/51 to 50/51, repeat 44/51 to 46/51, and the rollback. Do not
interpret the score movement as a valid candidate gain or reuse this exact
bundle.

## What remains valid

The audit does not erase executed observations. It preserves:

- official verifier scores, rewards, and property identities;
- candidate activation and Worker-trajectory observations;
- exact model, token, cost, retry, cleanup, and controller accounting; and
- controller, resume, rollback, protection-review, and artifact-retention
  infrastructure behavior.

It withdraws the causal and scientific interpretation that a valid reusable
harness caused those gains. It also prevents contaminated candidates from
serving as positive experience episodes in later retrieval.

## Required readiness gate

Before another main-scale search, every proposed candidate must carry a
predicate-level provenance review across prompt, tool code, descriptors,
validators, skills, memory, middleware, and routing. Each Worker-facing rule
must point to public task evidence or be removed. A declaration that the raw
diagnostic was hidden is not enough.

After that universal gate exists, run one bounded fresh target using public-only
Evolver evidence and a blind Worker. It must pass provenance review before
dispatch and produce a retained official improvement without semantic answer
projection. Until both conditions are met, main-experiment readiness remains
`NO-GO`.
