# Primitive-H0 trajectory bank and panelized global QRS evolution

Date: 2026-08-24

Status: accepted prospective method; local implementation complete; Primitive live protocol gate passed; mini-scheduler gate remains `NO-GO`

Supersedes: the detailed hand-authored S5 revisit and S6 deliverable-audit substrate in `2026-08-24-qrs-only-main-independent-ahe-and-quant-h0-s6.md`; it does not rewrite or invalidate the retained Core-v1--v4 construct results.

## Decision

The main QRS lineage will no longer start from the strongest hand-written six-stage workflow. It starts from a disclosed, deliberately underspecified **Quant-H0-S6 Primitive** substrate:

- the same Worker model, shell tool, recorder tool, context and execution limits;
- six named quantitative-research coordinates and observable structured transitions;
- a public-only and hidden-answer firewall;
- no hand-authored S5-to-S2/S3/S4 revisit rule;
- no hand-authored S6 deliverable-audit checklist; and
- no task, family, formula, estimator, threshold or expected-value patch.

The six stage bodies say only where work is being organized. The Evolver is responsible for discovering better stage content, cross-stage handoffs and end-to-end workflow policy from multiple public, answer-free Worker trajectories.

The intended outer experiment is:

```text
external exploratory Primitive-H0 selection
                    |
                    v
handoff one frozen Primitive-H0 identity and runtime
                    |
                    v
run fresh H0 independently on every development task
                    |
                    v
controller retains the complete H0 trajectory bank
                    |
                    v
family focus panel + cross-family anchor retrieval
                    |
                    v
one cumulative global-workflow proposal
                    |
                    v
exact Candidate Information-Set Review
                    |
             PASS only
                    v
fresh focus-plus-five-anchor parent/candidate evaluation x 2
                    |
 repeated focus gain + focus/anchor no regression
             /                     \
         PROMOTE               retain incumbent
             \                     /
              advance to next panel
                    |
                    v
freeze final endpoint
                    |
                    v
fresh matched H0 vs final QRS on disjoint sealed panel
```

There is one cumulative global harness incumbent. Panels are optimization minibatches, not separate family-specific final agents.

## External Primitive-H0 selection boundary

The experiment may deliberately choose a primitive seed with learnable headroom. That exploratory selection is outside the QRS scheduler and outside the cumulative candidate lineage.

The scheduler does not preregister, dispatch, rank or pay for the exploratory selection runs. It consumes one external handoff manifest naming a materialized Primitive-H0 Worker and one concrete runtime profile. Selection artifacts remain in their own exploratory record and never become Evolver evidence, Candidate Reviewer support, panel fitness, or sealed feedback.

The scheduler begins only after that handoff. From its first H0-bank Worker onward, the selected Worker directory, model route, session limits, shell and recorder bindings are fixed across the complete development bank, every candidate comparison and sealed evaluation. The scheduler cannot:

- reopen the exploratory profile search;
- switch to a different Primitive-H0 after observing the H0 bank;
- rerun selection to obtain a different starting score;
- alter the frozen Primitive-H0 between development and sealed comparison; or
- expose exploratory selection outcomes to the Evolver.

This separation makes the scheduler estimand precise: it measures automatic cumulative QRS evolution conditional on the imported Primitive-H0 initialization. Primitive-H0 is not claimed to be the strongest manually engineered quant agent.

## Frozen base-harness adapter

Primitive-H0 is the reference initialization for this experiment, not a base-harness implementation hard-coded into the evolution loop. The scheduler consumes a standard frozen-base interface. An independently authored harness can therefore be designed, edited and explored outside QRS, then admitted through a thin adapter without integrating that authoring process into the Evolver.

The adapter materializes:

- one concrete agent configuration and runtime profile;
- a declared Worker-visible system prompt and optional skill surfaces;
- the common shell binding;
- the passive six-state recorder binding;
- Worker-visible mutation surfaces that cumulative Candidate Review can enumerate; and
- one frozen handoff manifest naming the resulting Worker directory.

The imported harness must preserve the public-task/hidden-answer boundary, emit public work-state telemetry rather than private reasoning, run independently before an Evolver call, serve as the exact cumulative-review baseline and support fresh matched controls under the same model and runtime. Once admitted, the scheduler is initialization-agnostic: it builds the H0 bank, panel views, proposals, Reviews, fitness gates, checkpoints and sealed comparison from that imported base without modifying it in place.

This makes base-harness construction and QRS evolution separable experimental objects. A different compatible base harness can reuse the same scheduler, while a comparison among base initializations remains an external experiment. QRS results are conditional on the frozen base named in the handoff.

## Full development baseline trajectory bank

The controller first runs the same frozen Primitive-H0 independently on all tasks in the development universe. In the current frozen specification, the public upstream binary pool contains 57 eligible tasks after three construct-calibration exclusions. Twelve tasks are reserved before execution for the feedback-sealed main panel; every remaining task enters development, so `N=45`.

Every H0 task gets a fresh model conversation, Worker process, sandbox, artifact directory and verifier execution. No per-task H0 variant is permitted. The controller retains, for every task:

- public instruction and public metadata;
- raw Worker/tool trace;
- parsed S1--S6 state trace;
- final response;
- an explicit artifact manifest, including an empty set;
- all Worker-produced artifacts;
- Worker execution and process records;
- turns, tools, errors and timing;
- requests, tokens, cost and cleanup; and
- trusted official outcomes in a separate controller-only surface.

The complete bank is immutable. Failed or empty task attempts remain in it and cannot be replaced because their output looks inconvenient.

Official rewards and verifier leaf details are not part of the Evolver trajectory packet. The controller may use predeclared official scalar/vector fitness to select a candidate after execution; it must not turn failed-property identities, expected values, checker behavior or reference answers into Worker-visible policy.

## Why the Evolver receives panels rather than all histories at once

Retaining all histories does not mean concatenating all raw histories into one prompt. The controller owns the complete archive and exposes a bounded evidence tree. During a panel round, the Evolver can retrieve:

- the current exact parent;
- the current focus-family H0 histories;
- one frozen H0 anchor from each other family;
- answer-free traces from previously promoted panels;
- public claim inventories and cumulative candidate history; and
- pointers to raw materials that it explicitly chooses to read.

This gives each proposal a cross-family view without requiring all 45 raw traces in model context. Large families may later be split into smaller deterministic subpanels without changing the method, provided the partition is frozen from public metadata and the same global checkpoint rules apply.

The panel schedule has two levels:

1. a focus family supplies the dense local evidence and may reveal stage-local or cross-stage failures;
2. cross-family anchors allow a `workflow_global` hypothesis only when at least two distinct task families actually show a repeated handoff gap, and the five non-focus anchors become matched no-regression sentinels after Review `PASS`.

A single-family observation cannot be relabelled global. The active endpoint remains a single cumulative harness. Every panel formally re-evaluates one fixed task from every other family, so cross-panel protection is part of the repeated matched gate rather than a metadata-only checkpoint.

## Evolver global-workflow contract

The Evolver now has explicit structured fields:

- `workflow_scope`: `stage_local`, `cross_stage` or `workflow_global`;
- `involved_states`;
- `handoff_gap` with source state, target state, observed gap and intended handoff;
- `workflow_evidence` with task, family, trajectory reference and observed handoff; and
- `predicted_end_to_end_observable`.

When enabled by the evidence contract, a global proposal must cover all six states and cite at least two already-read answer-free H0 trajectories from different tasks and different families. A vague statement that the complete workflow should be more careful is insufficient.

For this main lineage, the frozen mutation surface is deliberately narrow:
`systemprompt.md` and
`skills/quant-research-six-stage-workflow/SKILL.md`. The Evolver may change
either or both to improve the complete global workflow, but shell, recorder,
agent configuration, tools, middleware, memory and routing remain fixed. The
proposal remains task-agnostic and cumulative. Broader harness mutation is a
separate experiment rather than an implicit escape hatch in this scheduler.

Workflow diagnosis is Evolver-side evidence, not automatic Worker instruction. Every rule that reaches the Worker remains subject to the public claim-provenance boundary and exact Candidate Information-Set Review.

## Panel lifecycle and automatic fitness

Each of the six focus-family panels performs one bounded round:

1. Build a trusted answer-free panel view from the H0 bank and accepted lineage.
2. Ask the Evolver for exactly one cumulative global candidate or `ABSTAIN`.
3. Materialize the exact candidate and its complete Worker-visible claim inventory.
4. Review both the parent-to-candidate change and cumulative H0-to-candidate material.
5. On Review `PASS` only, run the exact parent and candidate on every focus task plus one fixed development anchor from each of the other five families for two fresh repetitions, reversing arm order.
6. Promote only if every cell is valid and protocol-complete, the candidate has no native-reward regression on any focus or anchor task in either repetition, the focus-family mean is strictly higher in both repetitions, and the same focus task is a candidate win in both repetitions. Anchor gains cannot substitute for focus gain.
7. A legal `ABSTAIN`, Review `REJECT`/`INCONCLUSIVE`, tie-only gain, unstable gain or valid regression retains the incumbent and continues to the next family. Only provenance, snapshot, runtime, protocol, accounting, cap or sealed-boundary failures stop the scheduler.

The official native reward vector is therefore a controller-side fitness signal. It is not an Evolver evidence source and cannot be translated into task-specific Worker rules. Review `PASS` authorizes evaluation, not promotion. Protocol completion proves observability, not quant correctness. This gate supports the precise term **task-reward-nonregressing panel promotion**; it does not claim property-set or checker-level protection.

## Sealed main and H0's exact role

The all-task bank is **development** because its trajectories supervise search. It cannot also be called sealed evaluation.

The current specification reserves twelve disjoint public-metadata-selected tasks, two per family. Their instructions, traces, artifacts and outcomes never enter the H0 development bank, Evolver, Reviewer, candidate, task selector or dispatch decision. After panel 6 reaches a valid scientific terminal outcome, the actually retained incumbent is frozen. It may be Primitive-H0 or any earlier promoted proposal; the method never assumes six successful promotions. The controller then runs:

- fresh Primitive-H0 on every sealed task;
- the exact final QRS candidate on the same task;
- two independent repetitions; and
- reversed arm order within each task group.

Thus H0 appears twice in the method:

1. it generates the frozen development trajectory bank used to evolve QRS;
2. it is rerun fresh as the matched external comparator on the sealed panel.

Historical H0 scores are not substituted for either role.

## Metrics

The main result should remain easy to audit:

1. complete per-task, per-repetition official native reward vectors;
2. paired candidate-versus-H0 win/tie/loss;
3. task means within family;
4. an equal-family macro and its candidate-minus-H0 delta;
5. passed/total as a secondary diagnostic when complete, with normalized headroom closure only as descriptive appendix context;
6. protocol completion, invalid executions and missingness;
7. requests, tokens, cost, wall time and cleanup; and
8. the complete proposal-to-Review-to-fitness-to-promotion funnel.

Because the sealed panel has exactly two tasks per family, the equal-task mean and equal-family macro are numerically identical by construction; they are not independent evidence. Do not headline a pooled count of heterogeneous properties or treat 24 task-repetition pairs as independent statistical samples. The complete development curriculum supports mechanism and optimization claims; the disjoint feedback-sealed panel supports the final matched endpoint. Two repetitions do not establish broad statistical stability.

## Relationship to generic harness evolution

This scheduler borrows generic orchestration patterns without retaining a Generic arm in the final QRS implementation:

- [GEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19457) motivates saving execution trajectories, reflecting on them and preserving complementary candidates rather than learning only from sparse scalar reward.
- [MIPROv2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11695) motivates minibatch optimization with periodic broader validation rather than placing the entire dataset in every optimizer context.
- [Automated Design of Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08435) motivates a meta-agent that programs new agent designs from an archive of earlier discoveries.
- [AFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10762) treats executable workflows as the search object and uses execution feedback and structured search history.
- [Inefficiencies of Meta Agents for Agent Design](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1135/) reports that indiscriminately appending all prior agents to the meta-agent context can be worse than selective evolutionary history use, supporting controller-side archive retrieval rather than all-history prompting.

The QRS contribution is narrower and domain-specific: six observable Quant Research States, state-conditioned evidence retrieval and relation localization, global handoff diagnosis, an answer-boundary Reviewer, and cumulative quant-harness promotion.
