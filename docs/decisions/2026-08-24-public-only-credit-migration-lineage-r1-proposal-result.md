contract does not uniquely make it an error. The pooled count matrices are
$8\times8$ and include an absorbing Default row. Summing every pooled cell,
including 21 Default-row observations, yields 22,956. Summing only the seven
non-default starting cohorts yields 22,935.

The task explicitly defines the cohort default-rate denominator over
non-default starting ratings, but the `summary.total_transitions` field is
specified only as an integer. The contract does not say whether that summary
field includes the absorbing Default-row counts. Both 22,956 and 22,935
therefore have a public-contract interpretation. The observed difference does
not uniquely establish a serialization defect, denominator defect, or reusable
correction.

This matters even though the trusted prescreen retained aggregate 106/107
headroom. Co-occurrence of one-property headroom and a public numerical
ambiguity does not show that they refer to the same property or mechanism.

threshold, or routing rule would turn an underdetermined observation into a
task-specific patch.
